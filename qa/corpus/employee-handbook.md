# Employee Handbook

**Document status:** Current handbook
**Document owner:** People Operations
**Effective date:** 2026-01-10
**Version:** 5.2

## Working norms

The company expects employees to act with care, respect, and professional judgment. Employees should communicate clearly, protect confidential information, maintain accurate calendars, and raise blockers early. A flexible work arrangement does not remove normal attendance, collaboration, or information-security responsibilities. Managers are responsible for setting clear team expectations and applying them consistently.

## Working locations and time off

An employee’s assigned work location is recorded in the employee profile. A temporary change of location may require manager and People Operations approval, particularly when it affects tax, payroll, travel, customer commitments, or regulated information. Planned time off should be requested through the leave system before the absence whenever possible. A manager may ask for a handoff plan, but an employee should not be expected to work while approved leave is being used.

## Expenses and receipts

Reasonable, necessary business expenses are reimbursable when they follow the Travel Policy and have an itemized receipt. Employees must submit an expense report **within 10 calendar days after returning from business travel**. The report should identify the business purpose, dates, cost center, and participants when relevant. Missing receipts require an explanation and may require manager approval. Personal upgrades, fines, entertainment without a business purpose, and costs for accompanying guests are not reimbursable unless a written exception is approved.

## Performance and concerns

Employees receive regular feedback through their team’s review process. Concerns about harassment, discrimination, retaliation, unsafe conditions, or policy violations may be raised to a manager, People Operations, or the confidential reporting channel. Retaliation for making a good-faith report or participating in an investigation is prohibited. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every investigation.

## Information and device care

Employees must complete required security training, use approved accounts and devices, and report suspected incidents under the Incident Response Policy. The handbook does not replace more specific policies. When documents differ, employees should consult the document owner or People Operations rather than relying on an unofficial copy.

## Questions

People Operations owns this handbook. IT Support handles device and account questions. Security Operations handles suspected security incidents.
