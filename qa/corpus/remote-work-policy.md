# Remote Work Policy

**Document status:** Current policy
**Document owner:** People Operations
**Effective date:** 2026-01-15
**Version:** 3.0

## Document metadata

This is the current Remote Work Policy. It is owned by People Operations and became effective on 2026-01-15.

## Purpose and scope

This policy describes when eligible employees may work away from their assigned office and how remote work should be coordinated. It applies to full-time and part-time employees whose role, manager, and work location have been approved for remote work. It does not change the employee’s obligation to meet working hours, security requirements, or team commitments.

## Weekly schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely **up to three days per calendar week**. The remaining working days are normally spent at the assigned office or another company-approved workplace. Employees must agree on their regular remote days with their manager and record the arrangement in the team schedule. A manager may require an employee to attend in person for onboarding, customer meetings, equipment work, or a team event. Remote work is a scheduling arrangement, not an entitlement to change duties or working hours.

## Availability and collaboration

Employees working remotely must remain reachable during their agreed working hours, keep their calendar current, and join required meetings with camera or audio as appropriate. A delayed response should be communicated in the team channel. If an employee cannot work because of an outage or other interruption, the employee must notify the manager promptly and use an approved backup location when practical.

## Equipment and workplace

The company provides standard equipment listed in the employee’s equipment record. Employees must use company-managed devices for company data, keep screens private, and avoid public networks unless the approved secure connection is active. Family members and visitors must not use company devices. The employee is responsible for maintaining a reasonably safe, quiet, and ergonomic workspace.

## Exceptions and review

A manager may approve a temporary exception above three remote days for a documented business or accessibility need. The exception must include a start date, end date, and written approval in the team record. People Operations reviews this policy every six months. This current version supersedes earlier remote-work guidance unless a written exception says otherwise.

## Contact

Questions about eligibility or an exception should be sent to People Operations. Questions about equipment or secure access should be sent to IT Support.
