# Legacy Remote Work Policy

**Document status:** Legacy — superseded
**Document owner:** People Operations
**Original effective date:** 2024-03-01
**Superseded date:** 2026-01-15
**Version:** 2.2

## Original effective date

This historical policy applied from 2024-03-01 until it was superseded on 2026-01-15.

## Historical rule

Under this earlier remote-work policy, eligible employees could work remotely **up to two days per calendar week**. Employees were expected to agree on remote days with their manager, remain available during working hours, and attend required in-person meetings. The two-day limit applied to ordinary scheduling and did not prevent a manager from approving a documented temporary exception.

## Historical equipment and security expectations

Employees using remote work were required to use company-managed equipment, keep company information private, and avoid unapproved public networks. The policy encouraged employees to keep a current calendar and notify a manager when a connectivity problem prevented normal work. These expectations were retained in later guidance, although the current policy may use different wording.

## Historical exceptions

A manager could approve a temporary remote arrangement above two days when a business need was documented. The approval had to identify its duration and could be withdrawn when the business need ended. Employees were still responsible for meeting team commitments and protecting confidential information.

## Replacement notice

This document is retained for historical reference only. It was **superseded by the Remote Work Policy, version 3.0, effective 2026-01-15**, which permits up to three remote days per week. When this legacy document conflicts with the current policy, the current policy controls. Do not use the historical two-day limit as the current employee entitlement.

## Review and ownership

People Operations maintains historical copies to support auditability and to explain changes in working arrangements. A historical document may be relevant when a question asks what rule applied at a particular past date, but its status must be stated. Employees with uncertainty should consult the current Remote Work Policy or contact People Operations.
