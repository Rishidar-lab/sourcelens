# Travel Policy

**Document status:** Current policy
**Document owner:** Finance Operations
**Effective date:** 2026-01-20
**Version:** 4.0

## Scope and approval

This policy covers business travel booked or reimbursed by the company. Travel must have a documented business purpose and manager approval before booking, except in an emergency approved after the fact. The traveler is responsible for choosing reasonable options and submitting complete records. A travel approval is separate from an expense report; approval to travel does not guarantee reimbursement for every cost.

## Transportation

Standard air travel is economy class for flights of less than six hours and premium economy may be approved for longer flights when the fare is reasonable. Rail travel is preferred where it is practical and lower-impact. Travelers should select refundable or changeable fares when the expected business schedule is uncertain. Ground transportation should use public transit, a standard rideshare, or a rental vehicle appropriate for the trip. Luxury vehicle upgrades require written approval before purchase.

## Lodging and meals

Employees should use a business-appropriate hotel near the work site or event. The lodging nightly limit is **USD 220 before taxes and mandatory fees**. A higher rate may be reimbursed when the approved destination has no practical option within the limit and the reason is recorded. Meals must be reasonable for the location and business purpose. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an explicitly approved client event; even then, local law and company conduct rules apply. Tips should be itemized when the receipt provides them.

## Expenses and submission

Receipts are required for each reimbursable expense of USD 25 or more. The expense report must include the business purpose, travel dates, destination, cost center, and any attendees. Employees must submit the report within the deadline in the Employee Handbook: **10 calendar days after returning from business travel**. Finance may return an incomplete report for correction. Reimbursement is paid through the normal payroll or accounts-payable process after approval.

## Exceptions and questions

Exceptions require written approval from Finance Operations before the cost is incurred when practical. An exception is not a permanent change to the policy. Questions about a trip should be sent to Finance Operations, while questions about working-location changes belong with People Operations.
