# Incident Response Policy

This policy describes how Northwind Analytics detects, reports, and handles
security incidents. It is a synthetic document for demonstration purposes.

## Definitions

A security incident is any event that compromises, or attempts to compromise,
the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of company systems or data.

## Reporting Requirement

Security incidents must be reported within **1 hour** of discovery to the
Incident Response Team at irt@northwind.example. Delayed reporting without a
valid reason is a policy violation.

## Severity Levels

- Low: minimal impact, no data exposure.
- Medium: limited impact or suspected exposure.
- High: confirmed data exposure or service outage.
- Critical: widespread outage or large-scale data breach.

## Containment

The Incident Response Team isolates affected systems, preserves evidence, and
coordinates communication. Employees must not attempt to remediate on their
own without direction from the team.

## Post-Incident Review

A written review is completed within 5 business days of resolution. Lessons
learned are shared with the Engineering organisation.
