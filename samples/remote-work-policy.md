# Remote Work Policy

This policy defines how remote work is arranged at Northwind Analytics. It is a
synthetic document for demonstration purposes.

## Eligibility

Most roles are eligible for remote work. Customer-facing roles may have
additional on-site requirements set by the hiring manager.

## Schedule

Employees are permitted to work remotely up to **2 days per week**. Any
arrangement beyond 2 days per week requires written approval from both the
direct manager and the department head.

## Approval

All remote work requires manager approval. Employees should confirm their
remote days with their manager at the start of each month. Core hours of
availability are 10:00 to 15:00 regardless of location.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a stipend for a home internet connection.
Employees are responsible for securing their home network with a strong
password and enabling the company VPN.

## Security Expectations

When working from home, employees must lock their screen when stepping away
and must never leave documents containing personal data in shared spaces.
