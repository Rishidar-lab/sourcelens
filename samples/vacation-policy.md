# Vacation Policy

This policy supplements the Employee Handbook with detailed rules about paid
time off. It is a synthetic document for demonstration purposes.

## Accrual

Employees receive **15 paid vacation days annually**. Unused vacation days
cannot be carried over beyond the fiscal year-end except by explicit exception
granted by People Operations.

## Request Process

Vacation requests are submitted through the HR portal. Requests of more than
five consecutive days require approval from the department head in addition to
the direct manager.

## Public Holidays

Public holidays are counted separately from vacation days and do not reduce an
employee's vacation balance.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is handled under a separate policy and is not deducted from vacation
entitlement.
