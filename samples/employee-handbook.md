# Employee Handbook

This handbook summarises the core policies that apply to all full-time
employees of Northwind Analytics. It is a synthetic document created for the
SourceLens demonstration and contains no real personal data.

## General Conduct

Employees are expected to act with integrity and to follow all applicable
internal policies. Questions about policy should be directed to the People
Operations team.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 to 17:00, Monday through Friday, with a one
hour unpaid lunch break. Flexible start times between 7:00 and 10:00 are
permitted with manager approval.

## Annual Leave

Employees receive **20 paid vacation days per year**. Vacation requests must be
submitted at least two weeks in advance and require manager approval.

## Remote Work

Remote work requires manager approval. When working remotely, employees must
remain reachable during core hours. See the Remote Work Policy for the full
schedule.

## Company Holidays

The company is closed for the last week of December each year. Regular
holidays observed include New Year's Day, Independence Day, and Labour Day.

## Confidentiality

Employees must not disclose confidential company information to outside
parties without written authorization from the Legal department.
